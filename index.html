MEGALOMANIA

Support

Turn a small sphere in your hand and an immense structure answers overhead. Close conduits, shift masses and lay spans across a world-sized lattice.

Help

Drag the sphere at the bottom of the screen to turn it and tap a node to act on it. A single line at the bottom explains each new kind of puzzle the first time it appears. Drag the sky to look around, and double-tap the sky to recentre the view.

Subscription

The first five structures are free. Megalomania Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription that unlocks every structure after them, and it starts with a free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap RESTORE on the subscription screen.
