MEGALOMANIA

Privacy policy · Effective November 23, 2026

Megalomania ("the app") does not collect, sell or share personal data, and does not store it on any server. The developer runs no servers and receives no information about you or about how you play.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the structure you have reached), your sound setting and which tutorials you have seen are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the number of structures you have finished is sent to Apple's Game Center so it can appear on the leaderboard. Apple handles this under Apple's privacy policy. You can turn Game Center off in the Settings app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether your subscription is active.

No tracking, ads or analytics

The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/aliaksandrvoitov/megalomania-app/issues.
